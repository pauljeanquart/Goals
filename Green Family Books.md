# Green Family Books

These are books by David Green, who founded Hobby Lobby, and his son Steve Green, Hobby Lobby's president.

## David Green

David Green is the founder and CEO of Hobby Lobby.

| # | Title | Year | Notes |
|---|-------|------|-------|
| 1 | *More Than a Hobby: How a $600 Startup Became America's Home and Craft Superstore* | 2010 edition | His first book: the story of Hobby Lobby and his philosophy of retail |
| 2 | *Giving It All Away…and Getting It All Back Again: The Way of Living Generously* | 2017 | With Bill High (Zondervan): generosity and leaving a legacy |
| 3 | *A Generous Life: 10 Steps to Living a Life Money Can't Buy* | 2019 | A lifestyle of generosity |
| 4 | *Leadership Not by the Book: 12 Unconventional Principles to Drive Incredible Results* | 2022 | Leadership principles drawn from Scripture (Spanish edition: *Liderazgo no por el libro*, 2023) |
| 5 | *The Leader's Devotional: 90 Days of Biblical Wisdom for Honoring God in All You Do* | 2024 | 90-day devotional for leaders |
| 6 | *The Legacy Life: Leading Your Family to Make a Difference for Eternity* | 2025 | With Bill High |
| 7 | *The Legacy Life Devotional: Living a Life That Matters for Eternity* | 2025 | Companion devotional to *The Legacy Life* |

## Steve Green

Steve Green, David's son, has been president of Hobby Lobby since 2004. He is also the founder and chairman of the Museum of the Bible in Washington, DC.
